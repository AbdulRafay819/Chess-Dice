Dice Chess

Roll to move. Spend a six on a sniper's shot.

Takes material, aims straight

How to play

Every turn

  1. Roll. The die decides how many of a selected piece's legal destinations you are offered: a 3 shows three squares, a 6 shows six.
  2. Pick a piece. Reselecting it shows the same offer until you make the turn.
  3. Move. Click a glowing square. If a piece has fewer legal moves than the roll, all of them are shown.

Sniping

  1. A six is a choice: take the six-square move offer, or snipe.
  2. Aim. Pick one of your pieces, then drag away from it like a slingshot. Direction snaps to that piece's movement lines; pull further back for more slide.
  3. Resolve. Aim error and a seeded wobble decide whether the line stays true. A shot that veers hits nothing.
  4. Blocks. The first piece on the line stops the shot. A hit ends your turn either way; the shooter stays put.
  5. In check. A snipe must take the piece giving check. If no shot reaches it, sniping is blocked and you have to move to block, capture or step out of check.

Winning

  1. Kings. Moves never capture a king — it falls only to checkmate, or to another king's snipe. For every other shooter a king stops the projectile.
  2. Win by checkmate, a sniped king, or by eliminating every enemy piece. No legal moves without check is a draw.

Controls

  1. Camera. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, or use the Bird / Human buttons.
  2. Aim. Release below 4% power to cancel. Escape clears a selection.
  3. Menus. Tab or the arrow keys move between buttons, Enter activates, Escape goes back.

Play online

Your room

Solo practice. Create a room for a live opponent.

Rooms are validated by the same rules module the browser uses. Snipes are resolved on the server with a seeded wobble, so both players watch the same shot.

How the room works

  1. Create a room, then share the code or the invite link.
  2. Reconnect. Your seat is remembered in this browser, so a refresh or a dropped connection rejoins the same colour.
  3. Live. Moves arrive over a realtime channel, with a polling fallback when it is unavailable.

Settings

Feedback

Shake and flashes are proportional to the event and always fall back to a still frame when reduced motion is on.

About

Dice Chess is a variant: normal chess movement, die-limited destinations, and a slingshot snipe on a six. Built with Three.js, Vercel, Upstash Redis and Ably.
